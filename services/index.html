What we treat

Services

01

Oral hygiene

A checkup here includes an exam, a cleaning, and a look at the gums. Sealants are available for children when a chewing surface is hard to keep clean.

02

Restorative care

A cavity caught early is often a filling. A tooth with a larger break may need a crown or a bridge. You see the recommendation, and the fee, before treatment is booked.

03

Root canals

When decay reaches the nerve, a root canal can keep the tooth. The visit is numbed, and we tell you how long the chair time is likely to be.

04

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, bonding, and veneers are planned around the teeth you want to change, not a packaged smile. Bring a photo if you have one.

05

Dentures

A full or partial denture replaces missing teeth. We schedule time for the fit, and a follow-up once you have worn it at home.
